# Airnook &ensp;<img src="https://img.shields.io/badge/✦-A%20little%20room%20to%20breathe-8A9A82?style=flat-square" alt="A little room to breathe." />

Airnook is an independent creative space for thoughtful software.
We believe technology should feel **calm, useful, and human.**
Not everything needs to be bigger, faster, or more complicated. Sometimes, the best tools are the ones that simply make a little more room in your day.
We build with intention, keeping things simple, meaningful, and respectful of the people who use them.

### 🌿 Our Philosophy
- **Simplicity over complexity.** Less noise, more clarity.
- **Purpose over features.** Every detail should have a reason to exist.
- **Privacy by design.** Your digital space should remain yours.
- **Calm over distraction.** Technology should help you breathe, not overwhelm you.

We believe there is beauty in making things that feel just right.
